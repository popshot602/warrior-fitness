About Warrior Fitness

Warrior Fitness is a workout tracking and training app designed to help users build workouts, track personal records, monitor progress, complete daily WODs, and train toward their fitness goals.

Features

Workout Tracking

Log exercises, sets, repetitions, weight, timed exercises, and complete workouts.

Personal Records

Track PRs for weight, repetitions, estimated 1RM, workout volume, pace, and more.

Workout of the Day

Generate daily workouts and follow programmed training sessions.

Army Fitness

Track Army fitness events, performance history, and fitness testing progress.

Exercise Library

Browse exercises, movement instructions, muscle information, and demonstrations.

AI Coach

Get personalized training guidance based on workout history, goals, and available equipment.
