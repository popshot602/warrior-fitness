Common Troubleshooting

  1. Close Warrior Fitness completely and reopen it.
  2. Confirm you are running the latest available app version.
  3. Restart your phone if a screen or sensor-related feature is not responding.
  4. Check Android permissions if a feature depends on Health Connect or another device permission.
  5. Before clearing app storage or uninstalling, remember that locally stored Warrior Fitness data may be removed.

Health Connect Troubleshooting

  1. Open Health Connect on your Android device.
  2. Review app permissions for Warrior Fitness.
  3. Make sure the data categories you want to use are authorized.
  4. Confirm the source app or wearable is writing the expected data into Health Connect.
  5. Reopen Warrior Fitness after changing permissions.

Available Health Connect categories can depend on your Android version, device, connected apps, and the permissions you grant.

Workout Data & Local Storage

Warrior Fitness stores much of its workout, history, profile, preference, custom-exercise, note, and progress information locally on your device.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove locally stored Warrior Fitness information, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Report a Bug

When reporting a bug, include as much of the following as possible:

  • Phone model
  • Android version
  • Warrior Fitness app version
  • What you were doing when the problem happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • A screenshot if it helps explain the issue

Request a Feature

Feature requests are welcome. Explain the problem you want the feature to solve, how you would expect it to work, and why it would improve your training workflow.

Planned future areas include AI Coach, personalized training insights, instructional exercise videos, additional health integrations, and expanded workout programming.
