Warrior Fitness

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Warrior Fitness handles workout, profile, and Health Connect information in the current public-release design.

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Current release scope

Warrior Fitness is designed as a local-first fitness application. AI Coach is not active in the initial public release, and this policy does not describe any unimplemented AI data processing or paid-service features.

1. Overview

Warrior Fitness is a fitness and workout tracking application designed to help users organize training, track progress, build strength, improve endurance, and use supported Army fitness and Android Health Connect features.

This Privacy Policy describes the information Warrior Fitness uses, where that information is stored, and how Health Connect information is handled. The policy is intentionally limited to features currently described for the public release.

2. Information Warrior Fitness Uses

Depending on the features you use, Warrior Fitness may use information that you enter directly into the app, including:

  • Training profile information and workout preferences
  • Available equipment selections
  • Workout history and completed training details
  • Strength, timed-workout, running, and other training results
  • Personal records
  • Body-weight entries
  • Custom exercises
  • Workout notes
  • Army fitness test results and related tracking information

Warrior Fitness may also read Health Connect information when you choose to connect Health Connect and grant the requested Android permissions.

3. Locally Stored Data

Much of the workout, profile, preference, and progress information used by Warrior Fitness is stored locally on your device so the app can provide its core fitness features without requiring normal workouts to be generated by an online AI service.

Warrior Fitness does not claim that this locally stored workout information is currently uploaded to a Warrior Fitness cloud server unless a future version explicitly adds and discloses that functionality.

Removing the app will generally remove app data stored only within the app's local storage, subject to Android backup, restore, device-management, and operating-system behavior outside Warrior Fitness's direct control.

4. Android Health Connect

On supported Android devices, Warrior Fitness can integrate with Android Health Connect. Health Connect access is optional and permission-based. Warrior Fitness should only receive Health Connect data categories that you authorize through Android.

Authorized categories may include:

  • Steps
  • Distance
  • Active calories burned
  • Heart rate
  • Weight
  • Exercise sessions

You can review or change Health Connect permissions using Android and Health Connect controls available on your device.

5. How Health Information Is Used

Health Connect information is used only to support fitness, training, and progress features inside Warrior Fitness.

Warrior Fitness does not use Health Connect data for advertising, and Health Connect data is not sold.

In the current release design, imported Health Connect exercise sessions remain separate from Warrior Fitness workout-history entries unless the app explicitly adds that functionality in a later version.

6. Data Sharing

Warrior Fitness does not sell Health Connect data or use it for advertising.

This policy does not claim that Warrior Fitness sends your locally stored workout data to a Warrior Fitness cloud service. If future versions add cloud accounts, synchronization, analytics, crash reporting, AI processing, or other external data processing, this policy should be updated before or when those features are released.

Android, Google Play, Health Connect, your device manufacturer, or other platform services may separately process technical or account-related information under their own terms and privacy policies. That platform-level processing is not controlled by Warrior Fitness.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored Warrior Fitness information generally remains on your device until you delete it through available app or device controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior.

Health Connect records are controlled through Health Connect and the apps that have permission to interact with those records. Changing or removing Warrior Fitness permissions does not necessarily delete records stored by Health Connect or another source application.

8. Security

Warrior Fitness is designed to limit data use to what is needed for the app's fitness and progress features. Local storage and Android permission controls help reduce unnecessary data exposure.

No method of electronic storage or software security can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their devices with appropriate device-level security such as a passcode, PIN, biometric lock, and current operating-system updates.

9. Children's and Teen Privacy

Warrior Fitness is intended for users age 13 and older. The app is not designed or directed to children under 13.

Teen users may use Warrior Fitness for fitness training, school fitness programs, JROTC physical training, and similar activities. Users under 18 should use the app with appropriate parent, guardian, instructor, or program supervision where applicable.

Warrior Fitness does not require users to create an online account for the current release. Much of the workout, profile, preference, and progress information is stored locally on the user's device. Health Connect access is optional and requires permission through Android.

Warrior Fitness does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If future versions change how information is collected, stored, shared, or processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Warrior Fitness may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Material changes may be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date shown at the top of this page and, when appropriate, by providing additional notice within the app or website.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or Warrior Fitness privacy practices can be directed through the official Warrior Fitness support page.
